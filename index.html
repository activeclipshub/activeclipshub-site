A Windows editor for vertical gaming clips

ActiveClipsHub is a desktop application for Windows that helps a creator turn gameplay and stream moments into finished 9:16 Shorts, and then send a finished clip to TikTok only when the creator says so.

Project status: in development, used by its author. The TikTok integration is built on TikTok's official Login Kit and Content Posting API and is pending TikTok app review; until TikTok approves the app, public posting is not available and the app is limited to the restrictions TikTok applies to unaudited apps.

What the app does

  • Editing on your own PC. You pick a moment from a video you are allowed to use; the app renders a 1080x1920 clip with the face camera on top, gameplay below, a title card and word-by-word captions generated locally.
  • Preview before export. A quick preview is created first. A final clip is exported only after you approve it.
  • Optional TikTok connection. You can connect your own TikTok account through TikTok's Login Kit, review a finished clip, and either send it to your TikTok inbox as a draft or, when TikTok permits it for your account, post it directly.
  • You stay in control. The TikTok connection never uploads or posts by itself: every TikTok upload needs your explicit confirmation for that specific clip, and you can disconnect and revoke access at any time. Automatic or scheduled posting to TikTok is not enabled.

How the TikTok connection works

StepWhat happens
1. ConnectYou click "Connect TikTok". Your browser opens TikTok's own authorization page. You choose whether to approve. ActiveClipsHub never sees your TikTok password.
2. Account shownThe app shows which TikTok account is connected (display name) so you know where a clip would go.
3. Choose a clipYou select an exported video stored on your PC and review the caption and settings.
4. ConfirmOnly after you confirm, the video file is uploaded to TikTok using the official upload API.
5. ResultThe app shows TikTok's own status. A local export is never reported as a TikTok post.
6. DisconnectYou can disconnect inside the app, which asks TikTok to revoke the access token and deletes the tokens stored on your PC.

Permissions requested (scopes): user.info.basic (show the connected account), video.upload (send a draft to your inbox) and video.publish (post directly, when TikTok allows it for the account).

Where your data lives

ActiveClipsHub runs on your computer. The author does not operate a server that receives your videos or TikTok tokens. Details are in the Privacy Policy.
